The guide's menu pages explain a screen as a whole. These pages go one level down: every button, menu item, switch, tab, field, filter, column, badge and shortcut, and every text a screen can show, each with what it does, what it changes or sends, where its text comes from, where it leads, the errors it can show, and the same action from the REST API, the CLI and MCP.

The list of controls is read from the demo by a crawl, so a page always matches the screen it describes.

- [Sidebar and header in depth](./app-shell.md): Every control of the sidebar and the header, which every screen shares.
- [Inbox in depth](./mail/inbox.md): Every control and text of Mail > Inbox, an open email and its dialogs.
- [Email providers in depth](./mail/providers.md): Every control and text of Mail > Providers, the provider editor and its tabs.
- [SMS messages in depth](./sms/messages.md): Every control and text of SMS > Messages, a conversation and a message's details.
- [Verify in depth](./sms/verify.md): Every control and text of SMS > Verify, a verification's details and the services.
- [SMS providers in depth](./sms/providers.md): Every control and text of SMS > Providers, the provider editor and its tabs.
